# Relative endpoint-conversion fractions and stock composition

A: ratio of national initial versus observed-entry endpoint conversion fractions, each using its class PAS stock at interval start. R=1 indicates equal fractions, not equal origin area. First-interval ratios are missing because entry stock is zero. B: canonical b1985 initial shares in the complete endpoint-flow and observed-PAS-stock denominators; unresolved classes remain in those share denominators. Dashed/shaded 2020–2025 values are diagnostic. Values are not smoothed. The ratio divides summed areas, not mean cell ratios. The factorization of class-area ratios is algebraic, not causal attribution. Stock at t0 is not time-integrated exposure; no annual hazard, spatial adjustment or inferential test is estimated. Native source areas are unchanged.
