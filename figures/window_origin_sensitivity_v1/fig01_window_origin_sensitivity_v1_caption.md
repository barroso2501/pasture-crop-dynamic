# Figure 01 — observation-window sensitivity

Origin of PAS→TMP conversions and continuity of baseline pasture stock on 24,889 fixed cells, using annual coverage and baseline years 1985, 1995 and 2000. A compares identical calendar endpoint conversions; shaded/dashed 2020–2025 values are diagnostic and excluded from primary pooled comparisons. B compares the first four intervals after each baseline. Its horizontal coordinate is elapsed time to interval start: 15 years denotes the interval ending after 20 years. Every baseline's first positive-flow interval is 100% initial by construction. C divides current pasture continuously observed since the baseline by pasture stock at that baseline, at the same elapsed starts as B. Aggregated conversion shares are ratios of summed areas, not unweighted means of cell shares.

Rebasing changes origin attribution, cohort composition and available history; elapsed matching also changes calendar time. The shape of the curves and window-specific 50% crossings do not identify a process threshold, an artifact fraction, stationarity or a regime change. Observed entry includes re-entry; coverage gaps retain unresolved origin. Accounting and native categorical validation do not measure classification accuracy. The normalized panel has 472,891 rows, including explicitly labeled reuse of the two accepted early b=1985 intervals. Source: validated interval-summary v1; plot script 18f. Separate stock curves are descriptive, not estimates from a fitted survival model.
