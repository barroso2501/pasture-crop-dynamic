# Figure: first observed entry and observed re-entry, canonical boundary 1985

**English caption.** Observed pasture-spell origins of endpoint pasture-to-temporary-crop conversion across 24,889 canonical cells. (A) Summed five-year conversion area (million ha), partitioned into a pasture spell continuously observed since 1985, first observed post-1985 entry, observed re-entry, confirmed entry with uncertain earlier history, and unresolved current-spell origin. The two small uncertain components remain included even when visually negligible at this scale; their distinct areas are retained in the source tables. (B) Observed re-entry as a share of confirmed-entry conversion flow and of confirmed-entry PAS stock at interval start. These are ratios of domain area sums with different denominators. No conditional value is drawn for 1985–1990 because entry conversion and stock are zero by construction. Shading marks diagnostic 2020–2025; the final line segments are dashed. First observed entry is conditional on complete available observations since 1985, not first-ever pasture establishment. Pools across intervals do not deduplicate pixels; no annual hazard, significance test or causal trend is shown. Re-entry in source labels can also be affected by classification instability.

**Legenda em português.** (A) Fluxos quinquenais PAS→TMP, em milhões de hectares, preservando os cinco componentes mutuamente exclusivos. (B) Participação da reentrada dentro do fluxo de entrada pós-1985 e dentro do estoque correspondente em t0; os denominadores são diferentes. O primeiro intervalo não tem denominador positivo. Sombreado e último segmento tracejado indicam 2020–2025, diagnóstico. As duas classes residuais pequenas permanecem nas tabelas. Primeira entrada observada não significa primeira implantação histórica.
