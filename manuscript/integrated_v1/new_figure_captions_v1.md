# Captions for the newly rendered integrated figures

## Figure 2 — Fixed baseline pasture cohort

Endpoint composition of the fixed 1985 pasture cohort. (A) State shares of the same approximately 49.036-million-ha baseline population at the nine reference years. Shading marks diagnostic 2025. (B) 2020 compositions of the Amazon-associated and Cerrado-associated baseline cohorts, using each cohort's own fixed-area denominator. The legend's residual category sums other states, water, nodata, unexpected and masked observations without double counting. Whole-cell primary-biome assignment is not exact pixel allocation to biome polygons. PAS at successive endpoints does not imply uninterrupted PAS between them; the chart is not a survival curve. Source: accepted fixed-cohort summary, with original revalidation scope retained.

## Figure 5 — Process sums by primary biome

Consolidation (PAS→TMP), replenishment (NAT→PAS) and NAT→TMP endpoint area summed across the seven primary intervals, attributed by each cell's primary biome. Vertical scales differ among panels and totals are million hectares of interval conversions, not unique converted areas or net stock changes. The same pixel can contribute in multiple intervals. Biome assignment attributes complete cells and does not partition converted pixels at biome boundaries. Underlying Phase 7 tables match the accepted production hashes; no new regional model is fitted.

## Figure 6 — Metric-specific HH footprint robustness

Composite assessments of HH location, recurrence and persistence for three alternative grids and two temporal windows. Each cell reports whether all declared component criteria pass; 21/30 assessments pass. The first three columns use primary 1985–2020, and the last three include diagnostic 2020–2025. Sensitivity is an accepted scientific result, not failure of accounting or absence of spatial autocorrelation. Positive global association and local footprint invariance are different comparisons. Source: unchanged accepted Phase 8E assessment table; no new Local Moran inference is performed.
