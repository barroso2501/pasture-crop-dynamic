# Table 1. Estimands, populations and interpretation boundaries

| Question | Analytical object | Population and denominator | Main temporal treatment | Interpretation boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1: baseline pasture fate | Endpoint composition of a fixed pixel cohort | Fixed PAS-1985 cohort; each state divided by its unchanged cohort area | Nine reference dates; 2025 diagnostic | A return to PAS does not establish uninterrupted persistence; not survival or an annual transition path |
| RQ2: PAS→TMP origins | Current observed PAS spell at t0 and its entry-history subclass | All PAS(t0)→TMP(t1) area; subclass flow shares additionally condition on confirmed-entry flow, and stock shares on entry stock at t0 | Seven primary intervals; separate diagnostic/full pools; boundary sensitivity 1985/1995/2000 | Initial is observation-boundary continuity, first observed is not first-ever; entry-flow and entry-stock denominators differ |
| RQ3: intermediate pasture | Annual PAS detection between NAT and TMP endpoints | Complete/incomplete intermediate support retained separately; reported 6.51% concerns all summed endpoint area in the retrospectively selected high-NAT→TMP cell group | Four intermediate annual observations per five-year interval; persistence-rule sensitivities | Not a full-domain share and not proof of direct conversion when PAS is undetected |
| RQ4: concentration, trajectories and spatial association | Directed flows, frozen cell states, Global Moran and local HH/LL patterns | Full fixed domain or explicitly selected high group; support-specific graph and metric denominators | Seven primary intervals; diagnostic eighth map; repeated cell observations retained descriptively | High group is not a statistical cluster; HH in a signed index does not alone mean substantive consolidation dominance; no causal model |
| RQ5: scale/zoning sensitivity | Comparable aggregates, distributions and spatial footprints | Fixed footprint; canonical grid versus 10k, shifted 20k and 40k alternatives, with declared support and overlap-area weighting | Primary and full-observed windows kept distinct | Robust totals/global association do not imply invariant local membership or an objectively unique grid |

All interval-area pools can count the same pixel more than once. Full-cell primary-biome assignment is distinct from pixel-level partition by biome. Undefined ratios and masked observations are not zeros. Source: unchanged evidence matrix v4.4, accepted methods and `config/manuscript_integrated_v1/source_inventory_v1.csv`.
