# Decision 019 — Phase 9 synthesis and evidence rules

- **Status:** Accepted for implementation before manuscript synthesis
- **Date:** 2026-09-21
- **Applies to:** Phase 9A–9C

## Purpose

Phase 9 integrates the accepted analytical evidence. It does not reopen frozen
classes, rerun spatial inference, select results by statistical convenience or
introduce new explanatory models.

## Evidence unit

The unit of synthesis is a claim-level evidence statement. Each statement must
identify:

- research question;
- metric and analytical population;
- spatial and temporal scope;
- primary or diagnostic status;
- descriptive, inferential or sensitivity evidence mode;
- numerical evidence or explicit absence of evidence;
- robustness qualification;
- interpretation boundary;
- authenticated source record.

Evidence, interpretation and limitation remain separate fields. A validation
`PASS` records computational acceptance and cannot be translated into
substantive robustness unless the prespecified comparison itself passed.

## Temporal rule

The primary period is 1985–2020. The 2020–2025 interval is included in the
evidence matrix and final synthesis but remains identified as diagnostic. A
diagnostic result may reinforce, qualify or contrast with a primary result; it
does not replace the primary-period conclusion.

## Spatial rule

The canonical approximately 20,000-ha grid remains primary. Alternative-grid
results qualify the generality of canonical findings. They do not redefine
canonical classes or Local Moran clusters.

Combined-domain, biome-attributed and alternative-grid results must not be
collapsed into a single population. Whole-cell primary-biome assignment is not
an exact pixel partition by biome.

## Inference rule

The synthesis distinguishes:

- descriptive quantities;
- Global Moran inference;
- Local Moran/LISA inference;
- sensitivity comparisons;
- validation evidence;
- limitations and open evidence gaps.

No causal claim is permitted. No cross-date significance, trend significance
or cell-level independence may be inferred unless an accepted test directly
supports it.

## Evidence gaps

Missing accepted evidence is recorded rather than silently filled. The initial
Phase 9A registry explicitly retains two gaps:

1. substantive redistribution of the fixed 1985 pasture cohort; and
2. complete origin composition of pasture converted to temporary agriculture.

These are not treated as negative findings. They are tasks for evidence
extraction from already accepted accounting products before final manuscript
synthesis. Any additional computation must be declared as a Phase 9 amendment,
not introduced inside narrative writing.

## Output rule

Phase 9A produces a machine-readable matrix, a readable rendering, coverage by
research question, an open-gap table, authenticated source inventory and
validation record. Phase 9B may construct final tables and figures only from
accepted rows or from a separately documented gap-resolution analysis.
