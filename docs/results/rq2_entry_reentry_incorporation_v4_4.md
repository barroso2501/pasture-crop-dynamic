## Scientific consequence

The already accepted post-1985-entry component contains both first observed entries and returns to observed PAS. It therefore cannot be interpreted as entirely first-time pasture establishment. Even the first-observed subclass does not establish first-ever implantation before the 1985 observation boundary. The decomposition describes observed histories in the source series. Longer observable history, changing populations and classification instability can affect apparent recurrence; the analysis does not distinguish physical land-use cycles from every possible classification effect.

The stock compositions contextualize flow compositions but use different denominators. In 2015–2020, re-entry is 19.117876% of entry conversion and 15.853277% of entry stock at t0. Diagnostic values are 32.488051% and 17.906327%, respectively. These contrasts are not adjusted risks or annual hazards. Earlier stock-normalized endpoint fractions and boundary-sensitivity conclusions remain intact.
