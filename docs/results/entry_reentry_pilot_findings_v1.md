Each denominator is the stated accepted entry component, in summed hectares across the two cells. These are not proportions of all PAS→TMP flow, nor averages of per-cell percentages. The stock and endpoint-flow fractions describe different populations.

Observed re-entry contributes positively in both pilot cells and both intervals. The figures show why it is useful to separate that component before interpreting all post-1985 episodes as first observed entries. They do not establish a regional trend or compare hazards. The two cells were used for a technical pilot and do not constitute a representative domain sample.

First observed entry refers to complete observed coverage before the confirmed entry since 1985. It is not first-ever establishment. Re-entry requires earlier observed PAS. A gap can prevent an earlier-history classification even after a confident non-PAS reset restores current episode-origin attribution. Those uncertain-history cases were exercised by native fixtures.

The 2020–2025 interval remains diagnostic. Pixels may contribute to flows in different intervals; sums across intervals do not imply a deduplicated spatial area. The historical audit’s event counts are weighted pixel-years and can exceed single-year support because repeat entries are counted.
