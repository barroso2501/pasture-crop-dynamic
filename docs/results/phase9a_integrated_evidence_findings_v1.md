# Phase 9A — integrated evidence findings

## Purpose

Phase 9A assembled the accepted evidence from Phases 2–8 into a single,
traceable matrix organized around the five research questions. It introduced
no new inference and did not replace the detailed phase records. Its value is
to make clear which conclusions were already supported, which required
qualification, which were sensitive to analytical choices, and which questions
still lacked direct evidence at that point in the investigation.

## Direction of the integrated evidence

For the stock-and-flow questions, the accepted evidence showed a persistent
national predominance of pasture replenishment over consolidation, alongside a
spatially concentrated subset of cells with high NAT→TMP magnitude. Activity
was not temporally uniform: it concentrated in particular intervals, shifted
geographically, and displayed regional contrasts, persistence, re-entry, and
reversal. The Phase 9A evidence available for pathways supported only a
qualified statement: pasture was detected in part of the high-magnitude
NAT→TMP cohort, but interval endpoints alone could not establish the exact
pixel-level route.

Spatial analyses consistently indicated positive autocorrelation and
substantial HH clustering, with meaningful temporal persistence. These patterns
varied among biomes and were affected to different degrees by boundary rules,
multiple-testing control, spatial scale, zoning, and graph construction. The
MAUP analyses therefore supported neither blanket invariance nor wholesale
instability: some conclusions were stable under the prespecified tests, whereas
others were explicitly scale- or zoning-sensitive.

## Interpretation boundaries

The matrix is descriptive and associational. It does not identify causal
effects. A cell-level endpoint transition does not by itself prove a direct
pixel pathway, and lack of detected pasture does not prove direct native
vegetation-to-crop conversion. Results involving 2020–2025 retain their
diagnostic flag. Findings marked as sensitive remain valid evidence about
sensitivity and should be reported as such rather than suppressed.

## Relationship to later records

Phase 9A v1 remains the immutable record of the evidence state before the two
RQ1/RQ2 gaps were resolved. Phase 9A.1 adds the required cohort and age-origin
evidence. Phase 9A.2 will harmonize the English class label from **temporary
agriculture** to **temporary crops**, preserving `TMP`, the Portuguese source
definition, all values, and all accepted analytical decisions.
