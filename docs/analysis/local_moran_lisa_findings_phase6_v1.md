# Local Moran/LISA findings — Phase 6

## Analytical emphasis

The principal question for the three absolute metrics is where process areas
form significant spatial concentrations. Their primary result is therefore the
HH class. LL identifies structured absence or low activity and provides the
background against which hotspots are located. For the two C–R balance
metrics, HH and LL are equally important because they represent opposing
balance poles.

## Overall distribution

Across 40 metric-interval maps, BH identified 168,860 significant cell-map
records. HH accounted for 52,867, LL for 113,044, LH for 2,452 and HL for 497.
The dominance of LL in the total is driven largely by zero-rich absolute
metrics and must not be interpreted as dominance of the underlying process.

## Consolidation

Consolidation HH clusters occur mainly in the central-southern and southern
parts of the domain and change substantially among intervals. Primary-period
HH persistence for at least two consecutive intervals occurred in 1,298
cells. Mean adjacent-interval HH Jaccard overlap was 0.306.

The 2005–2010 map contains 10,998 significant cells, but 9,154 are LL with a
median consolidation value of zero. The visually extensive blue area in this
interval represents structured absence or near-absence. The 1,708 HH cells are
the appropriate focus for consolidation concentration.

## Replenishment

Replenishment presents the broadest and most persistent spatial structure.
There were 18,067 HH cell-map records, 4,354 cells with primary-period HH
persistence and mean HH Jaccard overlap of 0.389. These results identify
recurrent regions of relatively high replenishment.

LL was even more persistent: 7,068 cells had primary-period LL persistence and
mean LL Jaccard overlap was 0.688. Most LL distributions have a median value of
zero. They describe stable regions with absent or very low replenishment and
remain complementary to the HH hotspot analysis.

## NAT→TMP endpoint area

NAT→TMP HH clusters are fewer and more temporally variable than replenishment
clusters. Primary-period HH persistence occurred in 664 cells and mean HH
Jaccard overlap was 0.260. This metric therefore requires explicit examination
of hotspot appearance, disappearance and movement rather than reliance on a
single pooled footprint.

Large significant totals in 1995–2000, 2005–2010 and 2020–2025 are dominated
by LL cells with median endpoint area equal to zero. The HH counts in those
intervals were 652, 521 and 515, respectively. The 2020–2025 result remains a
diagnostic extension.

## Net consolidation–replenishment balance

The net-balance maps show a spatial opposition between high and low balance
regions. Both poles are substantive: positive HH supports a
consolidation-favoring interpretation, whereas negative LL supports a
replenishment-favoring interpretation.

Primary-period persistence occurred in 1,594 HH cells and 3,190 LL cells.
Mean adjacent overlap was 0.390 for HH and 0.340 for LL. The number of
significant cells declines to 1,437 in the diagnostic interval, compared with
values near 2,000–3,000 through much of the primary series.

## C–R balance index

The conditional balance index produced 19,358 HH and 14,695 LL cell-map
records. HH clusters show the strongest adjacent stability among the HH
results, with mean Jaccard overlap of 0.620 and primary-period persistence in
3,543 cells.

LL expands sharply after 2005–2010: 30 LL cells in that interval become 4,233
in 2010–2015 and 4,500 in 2015–2020. The diagnostic interval contains 4,314 LL
cells. This shift warrants joint interpretation with changes in active support
and observed index values.

Because the index is centered within active cells, HH does not by itself mean
consolidation dominance. The substantive label requires index > +1/3. LL
requires index < −1/3 for replenishment dominance. For example, some 1985–1990
Amazon HH cells still have negative index values and are only relatively high
within that map.

## Multiple-testing sensitivity

BY retained 72,221 of the 168,860 BH-significant cell-map records. The 42.77%
retention rate shows that the conservative sensitivity is consequential.
Primary figures may continue to use BH as prespecified, but highlighted
clusters should be checked against BY and described as BY-robust when
applicable.

## Main conclusion

The analysis supports a clear reporting hierarchy. HH clusters define the
spatial concentrations of consolidation, replenishment and NAT→TMP conversion.
Their persistence and movement are the central Phase 6 findings. LL clusters
of these absolute metrics describe structured low activity or absence. In the
C–R balance analyses, HH and LL jointly reveal the spatial opposition between
consolidation- and replenishment-favoring regions.
